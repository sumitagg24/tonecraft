# ToneCraft Brand DNA

> **Status:** Frozen — do not modify unless brand strategy changes.
> **Version:** 1.0.0

---

## Brand Essence

> **"ToneCraft transforms writing into voice."**

ToneCraft is not a writing tool. It is a language studio where every word carries emotional intent. The brand exists at the intersection of human expression and artificial intelligence — a place where writers find their voice, refine it, and share it with the world.

---

## Brand Personality

| Trait | Description | Manifestation |
|-------|-------------|---------------|
| **Confident** | Knows its value without shouting | Clean, restrained UI with purposeful accents |
| **Expressive** | Celebrates the art of language | Dynamic tone colors, fluid motion, editorial typography |
| **Precise** | Every detail matters | 4px grid, consistent spacing, intentional elevation |
| **Human** | Technology serves the writer | AI is a collaborator, not a replacement; transparent states |
| **Sophisticated** | Premium without pretension | Editorial quality, luxury accents, restrained effects |

## Design DNA Statement

> **"Every interaction shapes expression. Every pixel serves voice."**

This is the single sentence that governs every design decision at ToneCraft. When evaluating a UI choice, ask:
1. Does this interaction shape expression?
2. Does this pixel serve voice?

If the answer to both is yes, the decision is aligned with the brand.

## Artistic Direction

### Dominant Identity: Editorial Luxury (60%)

ToneCraft's visual identity is rooted in editorial luxury — the same craft principles that guide high-end publishing and premium brand design. This means:

- **Typography-first**: Headlines and body text are the primary visual elements
- **Generous whitespace**: Content breathes; the UI never feels crowded
- **Refined color palette**: Deep navy base with tone-specific accent colors
- **Quality over quantity**: Fewer elements, each executed with precision
- **Editorial hierarchy**: Clear visual structure that guides the eye naturally

### Secondary Identity: Apple (20%)

Apple's influence brings:

- **Pixel-perfect precision**: Every element is placed with intention
- **Clean interactions**: No unnecessary decoration; form follows function
- **Premium materials**: Surfaces feel tangible — glass, paper, metal
- **Restrained animation**: Motion that clarifies, never distracts
- **Accessibility as foundation**: Every interaction is usable by everyone

### Tertiary Identity: Arc Browser (10%)

Arc's influence contributes:

- **Contextual navigation**: The rail is a map, not a menu
- **Fluid workspace**: Panels and drawers that adapt to the task
- **Command-driven interaction**: Power users navigate with speed and precision
- **Clean chrome**: Minimal UI chrome that gets out of the way

### Accent Identity: Futuristic Motion (10%)

Purposeful motion that communicates AI state:

- **AI thinking**: Subtle pulse and cursor glow
- **Streaming**: Text reveals naturally, line by line
- **Tone transitions**: Accent colors shift smoothly
- **Knowledge attachment**: Cards flow into context instead of popping in

## Visual Identity Guide

### Logo Language
- The ToneCraft logo uses the brand name in Inter Bold
- The "o" in "ToneCraft" is replaced with a voice wave symbol
- The wave symbol uses the current tone accent color
- Logo is never distorted, rotated, or placed on busy backgrounds

### Shape Language
- **Primary shapes**: Rounded rectangles (radius-lg and above)
- **Secondary shapes**: Circles and ovals for avatars and indicators
- **Accent shapes**: Subtle curves in illustrations and empty states
- **Avoid**: Sharp angles, triangles, geometric complexity

### Illustration Style
- **Style**: Minimal, editorial line illustrations
- **Color**: Monochrome with tone accent color for emphasis
- **Usage**: Empty states, onboarding steps, feature highlights
- **Avoid**: Photorealistic illustrations, complex scenes

### Photography Style
- **Style**: Clean, studio-quality images with depth of field
- **Color**: Desaturated with tone accent color grading
- **Usage**: Marketing pages, case studies, testimonials
- **Avoid**: Busy backgrounds, cluttered compositions

### AI Illustrations
- **Style**: Abstract representations of language and voice
- **Elements**: Flowing lines, waveforms, particle trails
- **Color**: Tone accent colors with gradient transitions
- **Usage**: AI state indicators, loading animations, hero backgrounds

### Icon Style
- **Library**: lucide-react (stroke-based, consistent weight)
- **Weight**: 1.5px stroke for standard icons, 2px for emphasis
- **Color**: semantic-text for active, semantic-muted for inactive
- **Size**: 16px (inline), 20px (navigation), 24px (features)
- **Avoid**: Filled icons, multi-weight mixes, brand-specific icon styles

### 3D and Depth
- **Approach**: Subtle 3D effects only for premium-tier features
- **Usage**: Voice Orb component, premium surfaces, hero elements
- **Avoid**: Heavy 3D everywhere, parallax on scroll, excessive depth

### Glass and Texture
- **Glass**: Reserved for overlays (command palette, context drawer)
- **Texture**: Subtle noise grain on backgrounds (1-2% opacity)
- **Avoid**: Glass on primary content, heavy textures that distract

### Lighting and Surface
- **Lighting**: Simulated top-left light source for consistent shadows
- **Surface**: Matte finish with subtle sheen on premium elements
- **Avoid**: Harsh shadows, glossy surfaces, inconsistent light direction

### Backgrounds
- **Primary**: Deep navy (`#0B0F1A`) for dark theme
- **Secondary**: Off-white (`#F8FAFC`) for light theme
- **Accent**: Subtle gradient overlays for hero sections
- **Avoid**: Busy patterns, bright solid colors as backgrounds

## Brand Consistency Rules

1. **One accent color per view**: The active tone color is the only accent
2. **No purple defaults**: The old `#6C64EE` violet is deprecated
3. **Typography is the brand**: Let text hierarchy create visual impact
4. **Motion serves meaning**: Every animation communicates a state
5. **Whitespace is a design element**: Use it generously and intentionally
6. **Tone colors are systemic**: When a tone is active, the entire UI shifts
7. **Premium is restrained**: Premium effects are subtle, not flashy
8. **AI is transparent**: The user always knows what the AI is doing

## Brand Anti-Patterns

These violate the ToneCraft brand identity and should never be used:

- **Generic purple gradients**: The old AI startup aesthetic
- **Glassmorphism everywhere**: Glass is for overlays only
- **Neon glow effects**: Too distracting for a productivity tool
- **Heavy 3D scenes**: Breaks the editorial feel
- **Constant particle effects**: Ambient motion dilutes focus
- **Multiple accent colors at once**: One tone, one accent
- **Overly playful illustrations**: Undermines the professional tone
- **Auto-playing media**: Users control their experience
- **Decorative motion**: Animation must communicate something
