# ToneCraft — Vision

> Phase 1 · Product Vision
> Every AI agent should understand what ToneCraft is *before* writing a single line of UI.
> Companion to `MASTER.md` (§0–2) and `docs/vision.md`. This file is the creative source of truth — the rules live in `Design-System-v1.md`.

---

## 1. Vision

ToneCraft is an **AI-powered creative collaboration platform** where words are the material, voice is the craft, and AI is the co-author. It turns AI from a generic text generator into an *expressive instrument* — one that can write like you, for your audience, with your context.

**One sentence:** ToneCraft is the writing tool for people who care about *how* things are said — not just *what* is said.

## 2. Product Mission

Make reliable, human-feeling AI writing accessible to professionals, creators, and teams — with precise control over **tone, voice, and context** instead of opaque prompt boxes.

## 3. User Personas

### The Professional Writer (primary)
Copywriters, marketers, comms leads. They have deadlines, a brand voice to protect, and a "voice in their head" that generic AI can't hear. They need **tones that sound right**, citations they can trust, and speed without sacrificing quality.

### The Creative Operator (primary)
Solopreneurs, founders, content creators. They ship constantly across platforms (LinkedIn, X, newsletter, TikTok captions). They need **repurposing at scale** — one idea, many voices, many channels.

### The Team Collaborator (secondary)
Marketing/support teams sharing prompts, knowledge, and personas. They need **shared libraries, permissions, and auditability** — consistency across a whole team, not just one person.

### The Power User (tertiary)
Prompt engineers and workflow builders. They want **agents, automations, and deep customization** — but only *after* the core writing experience earns their trust.

## 4. Product Personality

- **Confident yet approachable** — professional credibility with creative freedom.
- **Playful precision** — deliberate creativity without chaos; a professional tool with personality.
- **Human–AI synergy** — machines that amplify human expression, never replace it.
- **Minimalist vibrancy** — bold accents on clean foundations; the accent always *means* something.

## 5. Emotional Goals

| User state | What ToneCraft should make them feel |
|---|---|
| Opening the app | **Inspired** — the canvas invites creation |
| Choosing a tone | **In control** — the voice is theirs to direct |
| Watching AI write | **Trusted** — the output is transparent, citable, correctable |
| Editing the result | **Empowered** — reversible, fast, forgiving |
| Sharing the work | **Proud** — it sounds like them, at their best |
| Day 100 | **Relied upon** — the tool has become part of their process |

## 6. Brand Values

1. **Craft** — every pixel and every word is deliberate.
2. **Honesty** — AI capability is shown plainly; citations are surfaced; limits are never hidden.
3. **Speed of thought** — the tool never slows the writer down.
4. **Individuality** — your voice is the product; the tool adapts to you, not vice versa.
5. **Calm confidence** — no hype, no noise, no rainbow clutter.

## 7. Design Principles

1. **Progressive disclosure** — reveal complexity only when needed.
2. **Contextual intelligence** — the UI adapts to the user's current creative state.
3. **Emotional resonance** — voice-driven design that mirrors the creator's intent.
4. **Trust through transparency** — clear affordances, honest feedback, coherent architecture.
5. **Every pixel justified** — size, color, spacing must serve a purpose.

## 8. UX Principles

1. **Rail-first navigation** — persistent, labeled destinations; never hunt for a feature.
2. **Three-second discoverability** — every capability findable within 3 seconds.
3. **Reversibility** — undo, regenerate, and rewrite are core workflows, not afterthoughts.
4. **Progressive mastery** — onboard beginners, empower experts, delight power users.
5. **Zero dead ends** — every empty state leads to an action.

## 9. Success Metrics

- **Time-to-first-write** under 60 seconds for a new user.
- **Tone accuracy** — % of outputs rated "matches my intent" on first pass.
- **Regeneration rate** — trending *down* as tone control improves.
- **Adoption of libraries** — % of weekly-active users with ≥1 saved prompt/persona.
- **Trust signals** — citation usage and share/download counts on AI outputs.
- **Retention** — Day-7 and Day-30 retention vs. cohort baseline.

## 10. Anti-Goals (What ToneCraft Is NOT)

- ❌ **Not a chatbot wrapper** — no generic "ask me anything" text box as the centerpiece.
- ❌ **Not a generic SaaS dashboard** — no blue-dashboard corporate neutrality.
- ❌ **Not a content farm** — no mass-generated, personality-free output as the default.
- ❌ **Not a distraction machine** — no autoplay, no celebratory confetti for every action.
- ❌ **Not a toy** — whimsy is allowed; unreliability is not.
- ❌ **Not an opaque black box** — AI work must always be inspectable and correctable.
